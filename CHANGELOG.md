# Jurisprudence Navigator: version history

Rule: every change, major or minor, bumps the version. Major (X.0) = new units or a new structure. Minor (X.Y) = added or corrected content, features or fixes. The app file is named Jurisprudence_Navigator_vX.Y.html.

## v2.3 (29 Oct 2026): iPad layout and text size
- Fixed: the app was locked to a 720 px column, so on an iPad it sat in a narrow strip. It now widens to 1040 px on tablets and laptops, and the module list shows two cards per row from 700 px up. Phone layout is unchanged.
- Added: A- and A+ buttons at the top right change the text size in six steps (0.9x to 1.6x). The choice is remembered on the device. Tablets start at 1.25x, phones at 1x.
- Fixed: unit numbers such as IV-c no longer wrap onto two lines.
- Home text updated: the line saying V-VI needs Veitch was out of date.
- No content change. 498 automated checks pass.
